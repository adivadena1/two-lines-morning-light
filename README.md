# Two Lines of Morning Light — a two-line micro poem

An entry for the [make.fun "Two Lines of Morning Light" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gudril-cool-two-lines-of-morning-light-ade94fc5).

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) writes one original
two-line micro poem about light and morning to `/output/entry.txt` — 101
characters including the line break, inside the 120-character gate.

> A pale square of morning light climbs the bedroom wall
> and finds the photograph I meant to turn away.

One image of light arriving (the pale square climbing the wall); the second
line opens it (the light finds the photograph), and leaves unsaid who is in
the photograph and why it was meant to be turned away.
